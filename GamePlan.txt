Snake Game — Development Plan

Tech Stack
- Language: Python
- Library: Pygame

Game Rules
- Snake moves continuously in one direction
- Player changes direction with WASD or arrow keys
- Snake cannot reverse direction (e.g. going right → can't go left)
- Eating food grows the snake by 1 segment
- Hitting a wall = game over
- Hitting your own body = game over

---

Stages

Stage 1 — Window & Moving Square ✅
- Open a Pygame window
- Draw a rectangle
- Move it around with WASD

Stage 2 — Snake Body ✅
- Replace single square with a list of positions
- Each frame: add new head to front, remove tail
- Snake appears to move smoothly across the grid

Stage 3 — Grid-Based Movement ✅
- Movement snaps to a tile grid (e.g. 20×20px tiles)
- Snake moves one tile at a time on a timer (not every frame)
- Prevents diagonal movement and keeps movement clean

Stage 4 — Food ✅
- Food spawns at a random grid position
- Head collides with food → snake grows (skip tail removal)
- New food spawns after eating

Stage 5 — Collision Detection ✅
- Head goes out of bounds → game over
- Head overlaps own body → game over

Stage 6 — Score ✅
- Score increases each time food is eaten
- Score displayed on screen using `pygame.font`

Stage 7 — Speed Increase ✅
- Every X points the move timer gets shorter
- Snake moves faster as score grows
- Cap speed at a maximum so it stays playable

Stage 8 — Main Menu ✅
- Title screen on launch
- Press Enter to start
- Shows high score

Stage 9 — Game Over Screen ✅
- Overlay showing final score
- "New high score!" if beaten
- Press Enter to restart, Esc for menu

Stage 10 — High Score ✅
- Save high score to a `.json` file
- Loads on startup, saves when beaten

Stage 11 — Polish (optional)
- Retro pixel art sprites for head, body, food
- Grid lines drawn on background
- Sound effects on eat / game over
